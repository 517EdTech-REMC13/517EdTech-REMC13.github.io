About 517 Ed Tech

A regional network for the educators of the 517.

We're a small crew of instructional technology folks helping schools across Michigan's Capital Region turn everyday tools into better teaching.

The most influential interactions in a school happen between a teacher and a learner.

Everything we do comes back to that. 517 Ed Tech helps educators invest their time, energy, and creativity into those seemingly-mundane — but wildly impactful — moments with students every day.

We launched in 2014 as part of the REMC Association of Michigan, and we've grown into a network of 700+ public, private, and post-secondary educators across Clinton, Eaton, and Ingham counties. There's room for you.

The team

Real educators you'll actually work with.

517 Ed Tech is coordinated by instructional technology staff from across the region's intermediate districts.
