Five Gemini prompts that save teachers an hour a week

KT
Ken Turner · Instruction & Technology Coach, Ingham ISD
February 18, 2026 · 3 min read

You don't need to be an “AI person” to get an hour back this week. Here are five prompts we hand out at gatherings — copy them into Gemini, swap in your details, and adjust the tone until it sounds like you.

1. Faster, kinder feedback

Paste a student's writing and ask: “Give three specific, encouraging pieces of feedback a 7th grader can act on, plus one question to push their thinking.” You stay the editor — Gemini just gets you to a first draft.

2. Differentiate any handout

“Rewrite this reading at a 4th-grade level, then again with sentence starters for multilingual learners.” One source, three versions, five minutes.

Keep it private. Don't paste anything that identifies a student. Strip names first, or use initials — the same rule you'd follow for any tool.

3–5. The rest of the set

  1. Parent email: “Draft a warm, two-sentence note home about this behavior, staying factual and specific.”
  2. Choice board: “Build a 3×3 choice board for this standard with options at three difficulty levels.”
  3. Exit ticket: “Write three quick exit-ticket questions that reveal common misconceptions about this topic.”

Want the full slide deck? It's in the Educator's Workbench — and we walk through all five live at the next gathering.
